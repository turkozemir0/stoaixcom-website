FAQs

Common questions

How does an AI receptionist work for an aesthetic clinic?
STOAIX connects to your clinic phone number. When a patient calls, the AI answers within 2 seconds in natural British English, identifies the treatment they're interested in, checks your live calendar for consultation availability, and confirms the booking — all in one call. It works continuously, including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.
Is an AI receptionist appropriate for high-value aesthetic patients?
Yes. STOAIX uses a warm, professional tone calibrated for premium private healthcare. Research consistently shows that high-earning patients prioritise the speed and reliability of a first response at least as much as personal touch. Being the first practice to answer — at any hour — is frequently the deciding factor in where they book their consultation.
Will patients know they're speaking to an AI?
No. STOAIX speaks in natural, professional British English. Patients experience a smooth, helpful interaction and leave the call with their consultation confirmed. Practices using STOAIX report no patient complaints or concerns about the nature of the interaction.
Does it integrate with aesthetic clinic booking software?
Yes. STOAIX integrates with widely used practice management and calendar systems. The service is fully managed — no technical setup required — and goes live within one week using your existing phone number.
How is STOAIX priced?
STOAIX operates on a monthly subscription — a fixed, predictable cost with no surcharges for out-of-hours calls or high call volumes. Request a demo to discuss pricing for your practice size.
