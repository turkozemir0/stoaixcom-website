What does an aftercare assistant do?

It follows a recovery plan your clinicians define: a check-in the day after treatment, the care step that matters on day three, a reminder when a medicine or dressing changes, and a nudge to book the follow-up visit.

When a patient replies, it answers from your approved guidance. If the reply describes pain, swelling, bleeding or anything outside the expected course, it does not reassure; it alerts your team at once and tells the patient how to reach you, or to call emergency services if needed.

Patients feel looked after, problems reach you early, and more of them come back for the check-up that protects the result.

How it works

  1. 01We load your aftercare plan

    Check-in days, care steps and warning signs per treatment, written and signed off by your clinicians.

  2. 02It checks in on the right days

    Short messages or calls that ask how the patient is and send the step for that day.

  3. 03Warning signs go to your team

    Any reply that suggests a complication is escalated to a person straight away.

  4. 04The follow-up gets booked

    Patients are reminded and booked for their check-up into your calendar.

The assistant introduces itself as your practice’s AI assistant at the start of every call and chat, and hands over to your team whenever the patient asks. Transparency is expected under APP 1 and APP 5 and keeps patient trust high.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an aftercare assistant cost?

STOAIX charges US$150 a month for written aftercare and US$250 a month for calls, with 1,000 messages or minutes included. There is no setup fee and the annual plan is 20% cheaper.

Does the assistant assess complications?

No. It does not diagnose or reassure. Any reply that suggests a problem is escalated to your team at once, and patients describing an emergency are told to call 000.

Who writes the aftercare guidance?

Your clinicians. We turn your existing aftercare sheets into messages and nothing goes live until your clinic has approved it.

Can it follow patients who travelled home abroad?

Yes. It writes or calls in the patient’s language wherever they are, and your team sees every reply in the CRM.

Does it help with reviews?

It can hand over to the review assistant once recovery is on track, so every patient is invited to review at the right time.

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans have no commitment, and the 30-day money-back guarantee applies.
