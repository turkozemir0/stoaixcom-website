What is patient lead follow-up?

Lead follow-up is getting back in touch with people who enquired about a treatment but did not book. A patient asking about implants or a hair transplant is weighing a large decision, often with several clinics, and the clinic that stays in touch politely is usually the one they choose.

Clinics know this, but follow-up is the first job that slips when the front desk is busy. The STOAIX follow-up assistant does it every time: a reply the same day, a check-in a few days later, an answer to the price or fear question, and a slot offered from your calendar.

It stops the moment the patient replies, books or asks not to be contacted, and every message and call is logged in the STOAIX CRM so your team knows where each lead stands.

How it works

  1. 01Leads arrive in the CRM

    Enquiries from ads, Instagram, WhatsApp, website forms and calls land in one list with their source.

  2. 02We agree the sequence

    How many touches, on which channel, how far apart, and what to say about price, finance and common worries.

  3. 03It follows up and books

    The assistant writes or calls, answers questions within your rules and offers real slots from your calendar.

  4. 04You see what converted

    Every lead shows its status: booked, still deciding, not interested. Hot leads are flagged to your team.

Rules and systems in Australia

General information, not legal advice. Check your own obligations with your adviser or regulator.

Spam Act and Do Not Call Register

Appointment reminders are service messages. Re-engagement campaigns are commercial electronic messages under the Spam Act 2003, so they need consent, a clear sender and a working unsubscribe. Outbound marketing calls also have to respect the Do Not Call Register.

Telling patients they are talking to AI

The assistant introduces itself as your practice’s AI assistant at the start of every call and chat, and hands over to your team whenever the patient asks. Transparency is expected under APP 1 and APP 5 and keeps patient trust high.

Privacy Act and health information

Health information is sensitive information under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. STOAIX processes it on your behalf under a written agreement; your practice keeps ownership and control of every record.

Frequently asked questions

How much does automated lead follow-up cost in Australia?

STOAIX charges US$200 a month for written follow-up and US$300 a month for voice, with 1,000 messages or minutes included. There is no setup fee and the annual plan is 20% cheaper.

How many times will it contact a lead?

As many as you agree, usually three to five touches over two to three weeks. It stops as soon as the patient replies, books or unsubscribes.

Does follow-up comply with the Spam Act?

Replying to someone who asked about a treatment is generally a service conversation. Promotional messages need consent, a clear sender and a working unsubscribe, and every message the assistant sends has them. Outbound marketing calls also respect the Do Not Call Register.

Can it call leads by phone?

Yes, with the voice option. It calls, introduces itself as your practice’s AI assistant, answers questions and books the appointment.

What does it say about fees?

Whatever you approve: fee ranges, what is included, payment plans and why a consultation is needed for an exact quote. It does not invent discounts or offers that Ahpra’s advertising guidelines would not allow.

How is it different from the reactivation assistant?

Follow-up works on new enquiries from the last days and weeks. Reactivation works on your old list: people who enquired months or years ago.

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans have no commitment. Success criteria are agreed in writing at setup, and if they are not met in the first 30 days you get your money back.
