What does the STOAIX CRM do?

It gives every enquiry one record, whichever channel it came from: who they are, what they asked, the full conversation and the stage they are at, from new enquiry to booked to treated.

Your assistants write into it as they work. When the receptionist answers a message or the follow-up assistant gets a reply, the conversation and the next step are already there, so your team never starts from zero.

Appointments sit next to the enquiry that produced them, and a simple summary shows how many enquiries came in, how many were answered and how many booked.

How much does the STOAIX clinic CRM cost?

The STOAIX CRM is free with every assistant. There is no separate licence, no per-user fee and no setup fee.

Assistants start at US$100 a month (appointment reminders, written). Most clinics start with the receptionist at US$200 a month written or US$300 voice.

Rules and systems in Australia

General information, not legal advice. Check your own obligations with your adviser or regulator.

Privacy Act and health information

Health information is sensitive information under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. STOAIX processes it on your behalf under a written agreement; your practice keeps ownership and control of every record.

Your practice software

Australian practices commonly run Dentally, Dental4Windows, Exact, Core Practice, Cliniko, Halaxy, Zanda, Nookal or a shared calendar. We connect STOAIX to the calendar or system you already use, and check yours in the intro call before anything goes live.

Frequently asked questions

Is the STOAIX CRM really free?

Yes. It is included with every STOAIX assistant, with no licence, per-user or setup fee.

Can I use the CRM without an assistant?

The CRM comes with the assistants; it is not sold on its own. The smallest assistant is US$100 a month.

Does it replace my practice management system?

No. Your clinical records stay in your practice system. The STOAIX CRM handles enquiries and conversations before and around the appointment; whether it can connect to your system is checked in the intro call.

Can my whole team use it?

Yes, there is no per-user fee.

Where is the data stored?

Data is processed under a data processing agreement, and your clinic stays the controller. Details are covered in the intro call and the agreement.
