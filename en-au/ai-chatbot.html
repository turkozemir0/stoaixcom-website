What is an AI chatbot for a clinic?

A clinic AI chatbot is a written assistant that answers patient messages on WhatsApp, Instagram and your website, holds a real conversation and books appointments into your calendar.

It is not a menu of buttons. It is trained on your treatments, price ranges, opening hours and the questions patients actually send, and it keeps to those rules. Clinical questions, complaints and anyone who asks for a person are handed to your team with the conversation attached.

Speed matters most here. A patient who sends a price question at 11pm usually messages two or three clinics. The one that replies first, with a clear answer and a time slot, is the one that gets the consultation.

How it works

  1. 01We connect your channels

    Your WhatsApp number, Instagram account and website chat are connected to one inbox in the STOAIX CRM.

  2. 02We write the script with you

    Treatments, price ranges, photos you are happy to share, booking rules and handover rules, in your clinic’s tone.

  3. 03It replies and books

    The chatbot answers in seconds, asks qualifying questions and offers real slots from your calendar.

  4. 04Your team sees everything

    Every conversation sits in the CRM. Your team can take over any chat at any moment, and the chatbot steps back.

Rules and systems in Australia

General information, not legal advice. Check your own obligations with your adviser or regulator.

Telling patients they are talking to AI

The assistant introduces itself as your practice’s AI assistant at the start of every call and chat, and hands over to your team whenever the patient asks. Transparency is expected under APP 1 and APP 5 and keeps patient trust high.

Privacy Act and health information

Health information is sensitive information under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. STOAIX processes it on your behalf under a written agreement; your practice keeps ownership and control of every record.

Spam Act and Do Not Call Register

Appointment reminders are service messages. Re-engagement campaigns are commercial electronic messages under the Spam Act 2003, so they need consent, a clear sender and a working unsubscribe. Outbound marketing calls also have to respect the Do Not Call Register.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an AI chatbot for a clinic cost?

STOAIX charges US$200 a month for the AI chatbot, with 1,000 messages included. Extra messages are US$0.04 each. There is no setup fee and the annual plan is 20% cheaper.

Does it work on WhatsApp and Instagram at the same time?

Yes. The same assistant answers your WhatsApp number, Instagram DMs and website chat, and every conversation lands in one inbox in the STOAIX CRM.

Will patients know they are chatting with an AI?

Yes. The chatbot introduces itself as your clinic’s AI assistant and passes the conversation to your team whenever the patient asks.

Can my team take over a conversation?

Yes, at any moment. When a team member replies, the chatbot steps back from that conversation.

Will it give medical advice?

No. It answers questions about your services, prices, opening hours and booking. Clinical questions go to your team, and anyone describing an emergency is told to call 000.

What is the difference from the AI receptionist?

They are the same assistant on different channels. The chatbot answers written messages; the voice receptionist answers phone calls. Many clinics run both.

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans have no commitment. Success criteria are agreed in writing at setup, and if they are not met in the first 30 days you get your money back.

Can it send SMS as well?

The chatbot answers the channels patients message you on: WhatsApp, Instagram and website chat. Outbound SMS for reminders and follow-up belongs to the appointment and follow-up assistants.
