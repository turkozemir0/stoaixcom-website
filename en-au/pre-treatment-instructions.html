What does a pre-treatment preparation assistant do?

It turns the preparation sheet patients rarely read into a short conversation at the right moments: what to stop, what to bring, when to stop eating, how to get home. Each message is based on instructions your clinicians have approved.

Patients can ask questions in the same conversation. The assistant answers from your approved instructions only; anything that needs clinical judgement, such as a new medicine or a symptom, goes straight to your team.

The day before, it asks the patient to confirm they are ready. Your team sees who has confirmed and who needs a call, while there is still time to act.

How it works

  1. 01We load your instructions

    Preparation steps per treatment, written and signed off by your clinicians.

  2. 02We set the timeline

    Which message goes when: a week before, two days before, the evening before, the morning of.

  3. 03Patients ask, it answers

    Questions about the instructions are answered; clinical questions go to your team.

  4. 04Readiness is confirmed

    Patients confirm they are ready; anyone who is not is flagged to your team the day before.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a pre-treatment preparation assistant cost?

STOAIX charges US$150 a month for written preparation and US$250 a month for calls, with 1,000 messages or minutes included. There is no setup fee and the annual plan is 20% cheaper.

Who writes the instructions?

Your clinicians. We turn your existing preparation sheets into messages and nothing goes live until your clinic has approved it.

Will it give medical advice?

No. It only explains your approved instructions. Questions about new medicines, symptoms or health conditions go to your team, and anyone describing an emergency is told to call 000.

Can it send different instructions per treatment?

Yes. Each treatment type has its own timeline and messages, and the assistant picks them from the appointment in your calendar.

What if a patient is not ready?

The assistant flags them to your team the day before, so you can call, rearrange or offer the slot to someone else.

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans have no commitment, and the 30-day money-back guarantee applies.
