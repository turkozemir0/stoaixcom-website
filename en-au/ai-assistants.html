Which AI assistant does a clinic need first?

Most clinics start with the AI receptionist, because unanswered calls and slow replies are where the most patients are lost.

Clinics with a full calendar but empty chairs usually add appointment reminders next. Clinics with a large list of old enquiries add reactivation. Every assistant shares the same CRM and patient history, so adding one later does not mean starting again.

Rules and systems in Australia

General information, not legal advice. Check your own obligations with your adviser or regulator.

Privacy Act and health information

Health information is sensitive information under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. STOAIX processes it on your behalf under a written agreement; your practice keeps ownership and control of every record.

Telling patients they are talking to AI

The assistant introduces itself as your practice’s AI assistant at the start of every call and chat, and hands over to your team whenever the patient asks. Transparency is expected under APP 1 and APP 5 and keeps patient trust high.

Spam Act and Do Not Call Register

Appointment reminders are service messages. Re-engagement campaigns are commercial electronic messages under the Spam Act 2003, so they need consent, a clear sender and a working unsubscribe. Outbound marketing calls also have to respect the Do Not Call Register.

Frequently asked questions

How much do STOAIX AI assistants cost in Australia?

Each assistant has a fixed monthly price in US dollars, from US$100 a month for appointment reminders to US$500 a month for reactivation or the Growth Manager. The STOAIX CRM is included free and the annual plan is 20% cheaper.

Can I combine several assistants?

Yes. Every assistant shares one CRM and patient history. Taking the receptionist, follow-up and appointment assistants together saves US$50 a month on written or US$100 a month on voice.

Do I have to change my practice software?

No. STOAIX connects to the calendar or practice system you already use, at no extra charge. We check compatibility in the intro call.

How do the assistants handle Ahpra advertising rules?

Review requests are neutral, sent to every patient the same way and carry no incentive, and STOAIX never republishes patient testimonials in your advertising.

Is there a free trial?

Instead of a trial there is a 30-day money-back guarantee against success criteria agreed in writing at setup.
