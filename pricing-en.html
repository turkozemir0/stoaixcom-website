FAQ

Common questions about pricing

What determines the price of the AI agents?

Each agent has a fixed monthly price. Two things set it: the work the agent does, and whether it works in writing or by voice. You never pay for an agent your clinic doesn’t need.

What is the difference between a written and a voice agent?

A written agent replies to messages from WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger and your website. A voice agent makes calls or answers incoming ones. Both use the same CRM and the same patient history.

What monthly usage is included?

Each written agent includes 1,000 messages a month, each voice agent 1,000 minutes a month. Anything above that is charged at $0.04 per message and $0.15 per minute.

How does the team discount work?

When the Receptionist, Follow-up Specialist and Appointment Agent are selected together, a discount of $50 a month on written or $100 a month on voice is applied automatically. On an annual plan you also pay 20% less on all monthly fees.

Is there a commitment?

Monthly payment comes with no commitment; you can stop in any month. The annual plan is 20% cheaper.

Can I keep using my existing CRM and clinic software?

Yes. STOAIX CRM comes with every agent, but we also connect to the CRM, calendar or clinic software you already use. There is no extra charge for integration.

How does the money-back guarantee work?

We agree the success criteria together in writing at setup. If they aren’t met within the first 30 days, we refund what you paid.

Is patient data safe?

Patient data belongs to your clinic. We sign a data processing agreement under KVKK and GDPR; the data is used only to deliver the service.

Do the prices include ad budget?

No. The price of the marketing agents covers ad management. You pay the ad budget directly to Meta and Google.
