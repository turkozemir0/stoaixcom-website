Questions clinic groups ask

Answered in full on a briefing call

Is this an AI receptionist?

Reception is one of the eight agents. An AI receptionist answers the call that comes in; STOAIX also runs what happens before and after it — demand generation, a callback within 60 seconds, multi-channel follow-up, dormant lead reactivation, filling cancelled slots, post-treatment surveys and recall.

How do you work with our clinic?

As a business partner, not a software subscription. The agents work alongside your front desk rather than replacing it, and hand over on your escalation rules — anything outside the treatments and pricing bands they are trained on goes to your team. Our strategists review the campaigns and a named account lead runs the monthly review with you.

Does it work with the CRM and clinic software we already run?

Yes — two-way sync with 24 systems including HubSpot, Salesforce, Dentally, Pabau, Fresha and Google Calendar, plus a webhook API. Nothing is migrated and nobody enters the same data twice.

How does it work across multiple sites?

Permissions and reporting are split by clinic, language and team. Each site sees its own pipeline; the group sees one consolidated record.

How is KVKK and GDPR compliance handled?

A data processing agreement, regional hosting and a full audit trail. Role-based access with SSO and audit logs controls who can see what.

How long does deployment take?

Four stages: audit, configuration, a supervised launch on one channel at one clinic, then scale. Supervised and reversible at every step — nothing is switched on all at once.
