No. Each module is a standalone AI tool that works on any clinic account — you can run Smile Design or Social Media AI without the STOAIX platform subscription.

Is there a free trial?

No free trial on modules. They are billed monthly and you can cancel anytime, so the shortest possible commitment is a single month.

How fast do I get access?

Instantly. Your module is active on your account the moment the payment goes through — no onboarding call needed to start.

Can I add modules later?

Yes. Start with one, add the second whenever you want. If you take both, the Clinic Bundle price applies from your next billing cycle.

How do I cancel?

One click in your account settings. No lock-in, no cancellation fee — your module stays active until the end of the paid month.
