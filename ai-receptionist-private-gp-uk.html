The Problem

For private GP patients, an unanswered call is a broken promise.

Private patients are paying a premium precisely because they expect immediate access. Every call that goes to voicemail — or rings out — directly contradicts that value proposition.

FAQs

Common questions

How does an AI receptionist work for a private GP practice?
STOAIX connects to your practice phone number. When a patient calls, the AI answers within 2 seconds in natural British English, identifies the nature of their request, and handles it appropriately — booking appointments, taking messages for clinical queries, or routing urgent calls according to your protocol. All calls are recorded and logged for the practice team to review.
Can it handle urgent patient calls?
Yes. STOAIX can be configured to identify urgent requests and route them to an on-call team member, an urgent appointment slot, or an out-of-hours service — while handling routine bookings autonomously. The triage logic is fully configurable to match your practice protocol.
Is it appropriate for a premium private GP practice?
Yes. Private patients expect immediate responsiveness. STOAIX answers every call within seconds and speaks in a tone calibrated for premium private healthcare. Patients experience a smooth, efficient interaction — their appointment is confirmed immediately, not after a callback. The experience reflects well on your practice.
Is STOAIX GDPR compliant for patient call data?
Yes. STOAIX is built with UK GDPR compliance in mind. Call recordings are stored securely under a data processing agreement, and data handling meets the requirements of CQC-registered practices. Full details are available on request.
How is STOAIX priced?
STOAIX operates on a fixed monthly subscription with no per-call charges or out-of-hours surcharges. Request a demo to discuss pricing for your practice size and call volume.
