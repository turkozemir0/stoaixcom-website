Rules and systems in the UK

General information, not legal advice. Check your own obligations with your adviser or regulator.

Advertising healthcare in the UK

Clinic ads follow the CAP Code: no misleading claims, no pressure on cosmetic decisions and no ads for prescription-only medicines such as botulinum toxin. Cosmetic interventions cannot be targeted at under-18s.

PECR and marketing messages

Service messages about an existing appointment are not marketing. Re-engaging old enquiries by SMS, WhatsApp or email usually is, so it needs consent or a valid soft opt-in, a clear sender and an easy way to opt out. We set up each campaign with those rules built in.

UK GDPR and patient data

Health data is special category data under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. STOAIX works as your processor under a written data processing agreement; your clinic stays the controller and owns every conversation and record.

Frequently asked questions

Can you get my clinic into ChatGPT answers?

Nobody can guarantee that. What helps is a site with clear, factual answers, consistent details across the web, structured data and genuine reviews. That is the work we do, and we report where you appear.

Do you guarantee first place on Google?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. We report rankings, enquiries and bookings honestly every month.

We work with most website platforms. If we cannot edit your site directly, we provide the changes for your web developer.
