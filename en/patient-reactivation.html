What is patient reactivation?

Patient reactivation means getting back in touch with people already in your records: enquiries from months or years ago who never booked, and patients who have not been back. They know your clinic, so they are usually cheaper to win than a new lead from an ad.

Most clinics never work this list because it is slow, manual and awkward. The STOAIX reactivation assistant does it in batches: a short, personal message or call that refers to what they asked about, an answer to their questions, and a slot from your calendar for anyone who wants to come in.

Campaigns only go to contacts with a lawful basis for the message, every message has a clear way to opt out, and anyone who says no is never contacted again.

How it works

  1. 01We clean the list

    Old enquiries and patients are imported into the STOAIX CRM, de-duplicated and checked for consent and opt-outs.

  2. 02We write the campaign with you

    What to say, which treatment to mention, which offer if any, and on which channel, within your local rules.

  3. 03It reaches out in batches

    Messages and calls go out at a pace your team can handle; replies are answered in the conversation.

  4. 04Interested patients book

    Those who want to come in get a slot from your calendar; the rest are tagged so nobody is contacted twice.

Rules and systems in the UK

General information, not legal advice. Check your own obligations with your adviser or regulator.

PECR and marketing messages

Service messages about an existing appointment are not marketing. Re-engaging old enquiries by SMS, WhatsApp or email usually is, so it needs consent or a valid soft opt-in, a clear sender and an easy way to opt out. We set up each campaign with those rules built in.

UK GDPR and patient data

Health data is special category data under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. STOAIX works as your processor under a written data processing agreement; your clinic stays the controller and owns every conversation and record.

Telling patients they are talking to AI

The assistant introduces itself as your clinic’s AI assistant at the start of every call and chat, and hands over to a person whenever the patient asks. Being upfront keeps you on the right side of the ICO’s transparency expectations and keeps trust high.

Frequently asked questions

How much does patient reactivation cost?

STOAIX charges $500 a month for written or voice reactivation and $1,000 a month for multichannel, with 1,000 messages or minutes included. There is no setup fee and the annual plan is 20% cheaper.

Is it legal to message old leads in the UK?

Promotional messages by SMS, WhatsApp or email fall under PECR, so you need consent or a valid soft opt-in, a clear sender and an easy opt-out. We check your list against those rules before anything is sent and only contact people you have a basis to contact.

How old can the list be?

Any age, but the older the contact, the more the consent and accuracy of the data matter. We usually start with the last 12 to 24 months.

Can it call people as well as message them?

Yes. The voice option calls; the multichannel option combines calls, WhatsApp and email for the same contact.

What if someone does not want to be contacted?

They are marked as opted out in the CRM immediately and never contacted again by any assistant.

How is it different from lead follow-up?

Follow-up works on new enquiries from the last days and weeks. Reactivation works on your older list.

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans have no commitment. Success criteria are agreed in writing at setup, and if they are not met in the first 30 days you get your money back.
