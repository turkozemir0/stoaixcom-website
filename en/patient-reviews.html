How do clinics get more Google reviews?

The clinics with the most reviews are not the ones with the happiest patients. They are the ones that ask every patient, at the right moment, with a link that takes one tap. Reviews then lift your position in Google Maps and give AI assistants something to quote when patients ask for a recommendation.

The STOAIX review assistant sends a short, personal message or makes a call after the visit, asks how it went and shares your Google review link. Every patient gets the same invitation, which is what Google’s rules and consumer law require.

If a patient mentions a problem, your team is told straight away with the conversation attached, so you can call them before a small issue becomes a bad experience.

Rules and systems in the UK

General information, not legal advice. Check your own obligations with your adviser or regulator.

GDC, CQC and the ASA on reviews

Ask every patient for feedback in the same neutral way, never offer an incentive and never filter who gets the link. Reviews must stay genuine; the CMA treats fake or incentivised reviews as unlawful, and the GDC expects dental professionals’ advertising to be honest and verifiable.

UK GDPR and patient data

Health data is special category data under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. STOAIX works as your processor under a written data processing agreement; your clinic stays the controller and owns every conversation and record.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Google review assistant cost?

STOAIX charges $100 a month for written review requests and $150 a month for calls, with 1,000 messages or minutes included. There is no setup fee and the annual plan is 20% cheaper.

Is it allowed to ask patients for Google reviews?

Yes. Google allows businesses to ask customers for reviews. What it does not allow is offering incentives or only asking happy customers, and the CMA treats fake or incentivised reviews as unlawful. The assistant asks every patient the same way and offers nothing in return.

Does it only send the link to happy patients?

No. That practice, called review gating, breaks Google’s policy. Every patient receives the same invitation. Anyone who reports a problem is also flagged to your team so you can help them.

Can we reply to reviews with it?

Replies on Google stay with your team. Reviews and patient details must be handled carefully; never confirm in a reply that someone is a patient.

Can we use reviews in our advertising?

Be careful. Professional and advertising rules limit how patient testimonials can be used. The assistant leaves reviews on Google and does not republish them for you.

When is the best time to ask?

Usually the same day or the day after a routine visit, and later for treatments whose result takes time to show. We set the timing per treatment.

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans have no commitment, and the 30-day money-back guarantee applies.
